# lead-magnet

Read first, every time: `knowledge/lead-magnet-playbook.md`, `knowledge/voice.md`, `knowledge/hooks.md`, `knowledge/examples/own-ats-post.md`, and `knowledge/taxwire.md` (what Pau already told us).

## Step 1 · Material (never skip)
Run `lm-coach` unless `knowledge/taxwire.md` already holds, for THIS topic: the reader and their scene, the pain in their words, the object, a sourced proof, a viral reference, the gate. ⛔ Never invent a pain, a number or a customer story. Missing = ask.

## Step 2 · Angle
Propose 3 angles on the topic (different lever each: pain, desire, FOMO, proof, contrarian), say which one you'd pick and why. Pau picks.

## Step 3 · The resource (it exists before the post)
Pick the support from what it is:
- steps or a playbook → a Notion page or Google Doc (Part 1 why / Part 2 the method in detail / Part 3 how to do it fast, with a demo banner after each part)
- a table or checklist → a PDF or a sheet
- a calculation on their numbers → a free tool (skill `lm-tool`)
Rules for the resource:
- An intro of 3 lines: who Pau is, what Taxwire does, one link.
- "⚡ The plan in 30 seconds" box at the top, so they know what to do even if they stop there.
- Really give (the WOW). Lock only what needs THEIR data or your team (the FRUSTRATION): "we run step 4 on your real orders in 15 minutes".
- A short demo banner after each part: "Book 15 minutes and we [do the locked thing] for you" + the demo link.
- Simple words, one idea per sentence, zero em-dash, zero jargon, no code blocks.

## Step 4 · The post
- Write `drafts/<slug>.md`: the "Hooks" box (5 hooks, 5 templates, one reason each, `✅ picked`), then the post.
- Structure that worked (see the ATS example): hook with proof + object → reveal in parentheses → small pain → what's inside `1/ … 5/` (really give) → the second piece of value → the soft line ("I put the whole thing in one guide, step by step. it's yours.").
- ⛔ No "comment X", no link, no "like/repost" in the text or the first comment.
- Under the post: "to confirm" (every number not yet sourced) and the target reader.
- Self-check from `voice.md` before showing it.

## Step 5 · The visual
Run `lm-visual`: write `studio/content/<slug>.json`, render, look at the PNGs yourself before showing them (text overflow, blurred card readable enough to tease, keyword readable at phone size).

## Step 6 · The flow (write it in the draft, under the post)
1. Post time + who replies to comments in the first hour.
2. The DM sent to each commenter (short, in the voice, the link, one question that qualifies: "how many states do you sell in today?").
3. Who gets a warm follow-up (fit: size, stage, US sales in several states) and what it says.
4. What we measure 48 h later: keyword comments from the target, DMs, demos booked.

## Step 7 · Deliver
To Pau, in this order: link or path to the resource, the post (with its 5 hooks), the visual files, the flow, the "to confirm" list. Then log what he changed in `knowledge/lab.md` (before / after): a correction that comes back 3 times becomes a rule in `voice.md`.
