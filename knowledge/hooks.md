# Hooks

Line 1 does 90% of the work. Always propose **5 hooks from 5 different templates**, name the template, one line of reason each, mark the one used `✅ picked`. The author decides.

## First, what is this hook for?
| Goal | Family | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Cultural hook: open on a famous name or a big event, then bring your idea | many impressions, diluted engagement |
| Credibility | Proof: a real result, a deal, a customer story | fewer eyes, much more qualified |
| Connection | Personal story, or someone else as the hero | few impressions, highest engagement rate |

For a lead magnet: **involve the reader** ("you can…") or **name a result they want**. Never open a lead magnet with a story.

## The templates
1. **Authority**: "We [action] [credible number] in [time]. And [impact]…" → "this $50M staffing agency built its own ATS. here's how."
2. **Conversation**: a real exchange, each line labeled ("CFO: … / Me: …").
3. **Personal story**: a personal truth that triggers emotion.
4. **Numbers**: "You commit the [7 deadly sins] of [action]:"
5. **Pattern break**: "Your [X] isn't the problem. [Real problem] is:"
6. **Curiosity**: "This is the [clearest proof] of [strong claim]."
7. **Contrarian**: "Stop [common action]. Do this instead:"
8. **The named gap**: "Everybody does [X]. But they miss ONE thing to really [result]." The reveal comes right after.
Variants: **the quantifier** ("Only 5% of SaaS companies…"), **the pain** ("Your sales tax is broken because…"), **the insight** ("New data shows…").

## Traps
- A hook the reader has to decode is dead. Say what IS, in the present.
- A hook that describes the mechanism is a summary. The hook opens the gap, the body fills it.
- A hook that gives the conclusion leaves nothing to read.
