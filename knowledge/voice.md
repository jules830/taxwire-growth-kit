# The voice (Jules's tone, for Pau)

Jules asked that Pau's lead magnets use his tone of voice, because it works. These are the rules, taken from his posts. Pau's own words and stories always win over these rules: the voice gives the rhythm, Pau gives the material.

## In one sentence
A founder who is in the action and tells you things straight. He states a fact, shows the mechanism, and gives you the method.

## The rules
1. **Lowercase at the start of sentences.** "I", acronyms (AI, SaaS, US) and proper names keep their capitals.
2. **One idea per line.** Full sentences of normal length (about 10 words), no robotic staccato. Short fragments: one per post at most.
3. **The hook states a fact**, flat: "this $50M staffing agency built its own ATS. here's how."
4. **The reveal comes right after the hook, in one line**: "(and it's a big part of how they're hitting $50M this year)".
5. **"I know" first, "how I know" after.** State what you know, then show where it comes from. The gap makes people read.
6. **Give everything you can in the post**, numbered `1/ 2/ 3/`, each line a concrete thing.
7. **Always a small pain**, in one or two lines ("paying for 100%, using maybe 20%").
8. **Authority through the detail of the mechanism**, never through a badge ("expert", "leader").
9. **End**: "I put the whole thing in one guide, step by step. it's yours." or a variation. Never "comment X" in the text (it goes in the image).
10. Small personal touches are fine: "→", ":)", a funny aside in parentheses.

## Never
- ⛔ Em-dash (—) or en-dash (–). Use commas, periods, parentheses.
- ⛔ "It's not X, it's Y" / "X, not Y". It sounds like AI.
- ⛔ "Here's the thing", "moreover", "furthermore", "game-changer", "unlock", tricolons, inspirational LinkedIn tone.
- ⛔ Emojis at the start of lines. Questions at the end asking for opinions ("what do you think?").
- ⛔ A number you can't source. Every number comes from Pau or from a public source, or it goes in "to confirm" under the draft.
- ⛔ Links in the post body (they kill reach). The resource link goes in DM.
- ⛔ Any instruction to comment, like or repost in the text or in the first comment.

## Self-check before delivering
- Would someone who doesn't know Taxwire understand line 1 in one read?
- Does the reader leave with something they can do tomorrow, even without commenting?
- Is there one real fact from Pau (a call, a number, a thing built, a mistake)?
- Is every number sourced?
- Zero em-dash, zero "not X, it's Y", zero comment instruction in the text?
